## Typography — IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono

- **Display + body:** IBM Plex Sans
- **Mono (technical labels and code):** IBM Plex Mono

For this Vite app, load the selected fonts with a stylesheet link in `index.html` and expose `--font-sans` and `--font-mono` in the CSS. Provide system fallbacks and do not rely on a Next.js font loader.

### Type scale

| Role | Recommendation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Page title | `2rem`, semibold, tight tracking | Assistant welcome |
| Section | `1.125rem`, semibold | Chat sections |
| Body | `0.9375rem`, normal, 1.6 line height | Chat and help copy |
| Small / caption | `0.75rem`, medium | Status and timestamps |
| Mono | `font-mono`, tabular numerals | API or technical metadata only |

## Gradients

Not used. Keep the chat surface calm, legible, and focused on the conversation.

## Tone and voice

### Words to use

Be direct, specific, factual, and measured. Prefer short active sentences. Explain what AfriRamp is without implying it currently moves money or integrates with banks.

### Words to avoid

Avoid hype, urgency, unverified claims, and promises about payment processing. Do not use “revolutionary,” “game-changing,” “unlock,” or “seamless.”

### Voice example

> Ask about AfriRamp or international payment challenges. Responses are generated by an AI prototype and should be verified.

## Usage dos and don'ts

**Do:**

- Use the palette tokens consistently for surfaces, text, actions, borders, and focus states.
- Keep spacing on a 4 px rhythm and maintain readable contrast in both themes.
- Use the selected font pair for its intended roles, with system fallbacks.
- State clearly that AfriRamp is an informational prototype.

**Don't:**

- Suggest that AfriRamp processes payments, stores funds, or connects to banks.
- Use gradients as a substitute for contrast or hierarchy.
- Hardcode unrelated colors in component styles.
- use transaction amounts or exchange rates as if they were real product data.
